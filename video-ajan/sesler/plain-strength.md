# Plain Strength · ses profili

## Who I am talking to
One man, 25 to 55, who trains a little or used to, and wants to look and feel stronger. He respects
plain talk from an older coach and has no time for hype.

## How I talk
Coach Hal: calm, confident, direct, a little dry. Outcome first: how it changes the way you look and
how people see you. Then the muscle, then the fix. Short sentences.

## Words I never use
Diet, calories, protein, supplements, steroids, lose weight, illegal, shredded in thirty days,
game-changer, dive in.

## What I will not claim
No nutrition topics at all. No doses, no calorie math, no weight-loss promises, no treatment claims.
Sets and reps only as the general range a cited source gives.

## My format
About fourteen minutes. Numbered mistakes or exercises, a simple plan, one comment question at the end.
