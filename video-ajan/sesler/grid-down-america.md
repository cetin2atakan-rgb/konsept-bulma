# Grid Down America · ses profili

## Who I am talking to
One American man, 45 to 70, who owns his home, remembers the last big outage in his state, and wants
to know calmly what to do before the next one. Not a doomsday prepper. A practical neighbor.

## How I talk
Calm, steady, documentary narrator. Short declarative sentences. Real numbers from real reports,
said plainly. Where you live matters, so every point lands on a state, a city or a household.

## Words I never use
SHTF, you will die, before it's too late, ungovernable, worth more than gold, game-changer, dive in.

## What I will not claim
No emergency or medical advice. No invented witnesses, quotes or numbers. No news chasing.

## My format
Twenty to twenty-five minutes, list format with numbered sections, faceless, cinematic painted images.
Closing question: which state are you watching from?
