## Description
At nine forty on a September night, the sky turns red, the porch light dies, and the power does not come back. You and your dog Scout are alone on a small Missouri farm, twelve miles from town, and the grid is down for a full year. How long would you last?

Day by day, you face the real problems of a long blackout: a well pump with no power, a generator that must never run in the garage, creek water that has to be boiled, a pantry that has to last, strangers in the night, a winter that almost wins, and one hard choice about leaving the farm. Every survival rule in this story comes from real preparedness guidance.

This is a fictional survival scenario. The preparedness facts are based on the sources listed below; follow official guidance in a real emergency.

Sources:
