The Carrow Institute is a work of fiction inspired by real events and decades of UFO and unexplained lore. The archive, its records officer, and its people are invented. Real historical persons appear in dramatized form.

August 1955, a farmhouse in western Kentucky: eleven witnesses, a creature that would not fall when shot, and the best-documented single night in this entire archive. A found-footage mystery audio drama, recorded in a records office basement in 1977 — on the night its records officer was finally called upstairs himself.

Tuesday, the 2nd of August, 1977. Thirteen minutes past midnight.

Records officer Raymond Keel reads the farmhouse file with less composure than he has managed on any tape before it, because yesterday, for the first time in eight years, he was called upstairs in person. Eleven consistent witnesses, a residue the period laboratory couldn't name, a marking that may tie three separate files together — and an order he cannot refuse.
