## Description
A fictional found-footage audio drama inspired by the 1952 Flatwoods Monster sighting in Braxton County, West Virginia.
Seven witnesses, questioned separately, described the same hooded shape and the same metallic smell. Records officer Raymond Keel reads the file in 1977.

Saturday, the 23rd of July, 1977. Twelve minutes past midnight.

File B-037. A county illustrator's sketch of a tall, hooded shape with two lit ovals where a face should be. A twelve-foot circle of grass laid over in a spiral, "swirled rather than crushed." A residue card with a catalogue number that appears in no public listing, and a dried grass sample that still smelled of sulfur eleven months later. Keel gives the official explanation, a meteor and a startled barn owl, its full due before telling you, carefully, why it does not account for everything in the file.

What do you think came down on that hillside? Tell Keel in the comments; he reads the box, not the internet, but we pass things along.
