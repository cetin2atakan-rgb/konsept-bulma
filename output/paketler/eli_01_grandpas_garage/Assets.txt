## Description
A valve lapping stick, a points file, a hot patch you lit with a match: 25 strange tools from an old American garage and exactly what each one was for.
Some disappeared because cars changed, some because nobody remembered how to use them, and a few are still the best tool for the job.

From the service station spark plug cleaner and the timing light to the bumper jack, the hand crank, the post drill and the treadle grindstone, this is the garage and farm shop your father and grandfather worked in, one drawer at a time.

Which of these did you actually use, and which one is still hanging in your garage? Tell me in the comments. If you have a tool nobody can identify, describe it and we'll try to figure it out together.
