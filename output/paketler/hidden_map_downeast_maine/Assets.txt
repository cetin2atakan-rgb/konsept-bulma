## DESCRIPTION
Off the coast of Washington County, Maine, is an island that both the United States and Canada still claim. Between Eastport and Deer Island the tide forms the Old Sow, the largest tidal whirlpool in the Western Hemisphere, and in the woods near Cutler the Navy runs 26 towers, some close to 1,000 feet tall, that send radio signals to submarines underwater. Retired geography teacher Walter Hale counts down 25 geographic facts about Downeast Maine: the Bay of Fundy tides, the first naval battle of the Revolution, Roosevelt's Campobello, the last herring smokehouse in the country, baby eels worth more than $2,000 a pound, and the headland that is the first place in the US to see the sunrise for half the year.
