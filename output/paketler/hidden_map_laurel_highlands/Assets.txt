## DESCRIPTION
Under Chestnut Ridge in southwestern Pennsylvania, about four miles of cave passages have just become Laurel Caverns State Park, the state's first underground state park. Retired geography teacher Walter Hale counts down 25 geographic facts about the Laurel Highlands, the stretch of the Alleghenies between Pittsburgh and the Maryland line: Fallingwater built right over Bear Run, the falls at Ohiopyle that turned back a young George Washington, his only surrender at Fort Necessity, the 1889 Johnstown Flood and the incline built so the city could escape the next one, forty thousand coke ovens around Connellsville, Mount Davis (the roof of Pennsylvania), and Latrobe, the small town that gave the country Fred Rogers, Arnold Palmer and the banana split.
