## Description
The complete history of Apple, from a circuit board in a Los Altos garage in 1976 to the most valuable company in the world.
Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak built the Apple II, Jobs was forced out of his own company in 1985, and by 1997 Apple was about 90 days from running out of cash.

This episode covers the whole story: Ronald Wayne selling his 10% stake for $800, the Apple II and the 1980 IPO, John Sculley and the "sugar water" pitch, the Macintosh and the 1984 Super Bowl ad, the board fight that removed Jobs, NeXT and Pixar, the Newton flop, Microsoft's $150 million rescue, and the iMac, iPod and iPhone that followed. It ends with Tim Cook, the first $1 trillion valuation, and the criticism that comes with Apple's obsession with control: supplier factories, the Samsung patent war, the e-book price-fixing case, and the closed App Store.
