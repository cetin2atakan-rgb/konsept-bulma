## DESCRIPTION
A few miles from the Cumberland Gap, the town of Middlesboro, Kentucky, is laid out on the floor of an ancient meteorite impact crater roughly three to four miles across, and from Pinnacle Overlook you can look straight down into it. Retired geography teacher Walter Hale counts down 25 geographic facts about Cumberland Gap National Historical Park, where Kentucky, Tennessee and Virginia meet. It covers the fault line that broke an unbroken sandstone wall, the buffalo trace that became Daniel Boone's Wilderness Road, the 200,000 to 300,000 settlers who funneled through one notch, Tri-State Peak, the Civil War forts both armies held, Hensley Settlement on top of the mountain, and the highway tunnel built so the historic saddle could finally be left alone.
