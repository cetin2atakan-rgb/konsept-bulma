## Description
25 facts about West Virginia's Eastern Panhandle: Harpers Ferry, Shepherdstown, Martinsburg, Charles Town and Berkeley Springs, from John Brown's raid to the birthplace of interchangeable parts.
Berkeley, Jefferson and Morgan counties drain toward the Atlantic while most of West Virginia drains toward the Ohio River, and the region looks east to Washington rather than west to the coalfields.

Retired geography teacher Walter Hale counts down twenty-five facts: the record 1936 flood at Harpers Ferry, Storer College on Camp Hill, James Rumsey's 1787 steamboat, John Brown's Fort and its travels to a World's Fair, the courthouse that held two treason trials, the bloodiest battle on West Virginia soil, the Martinsburg roundhouse where the Great Railroad Strike of 1877 began, the country's oldest spa town, Berkeley Castle, Hall's Rifle Works, the Appalachian Trail's symbolic halfway point, and the view Thomas Jefferson called worth a voyage across the Atlantic.
