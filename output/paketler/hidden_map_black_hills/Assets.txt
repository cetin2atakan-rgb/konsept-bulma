## DESCRIPTION
In 1974 a bulldozer grading a hillside in Hot Springs, South Dakota, cut into an ancient sinkhole and found mammoth bones. At least 61 mammoths are now known to have slipped into that spring-fed pond and never climbed back out. Retired geography teacher Walter Hale counts down 25 geographic facts about the Black Hills: Black Elk Peak, the highest point east of the Rockies; Mount Rushmore and the still-unfinished Crazy Horse Memorial; Jewel Cave and Wind Cave, two of the longest caves on Earth; a gold mine more than a mile deep that now hunts dark matter; Pactola Reservoir with a town under it; the 1972 Rapid City flood; and the 1868 Fort Laramie Treaty that the 1874 gold rush broke. Under all of it is a dome of rock more than a billion years older than the Rockies people mistake it for.
