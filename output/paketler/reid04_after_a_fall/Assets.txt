## DESCRIPTION
#FallPrevention #SeniorHealth #HealthyAging

Most people check themselves after a fall, decide nothing's broken, and move on. In this video, we examine 5 warning signs after a fall that seniors commonly miss, including walking despite a possible fracture, a growing bruise, a delayed headache, new leg swelling, and the fear-of-falling cycle that quietly raises future risk.

You'll learn about impacted hip fractures, delayed subdural hematoma, bruising that keeps spreading, deep vein thrombosis after immobility, the muscle-pump effect, and the fear-of-falling cycle — and why each of these can show up hours or days after a fall feels "fine."

You'll also learn two protective habits — a 48-to-72-hour watch window and a graded return to activity — that take almost no time. If you experience a worsening headache, confusion, repeated vomiting, or new weakness after any head bump, these may be reasons to seek medical care immediately rather than wait.
