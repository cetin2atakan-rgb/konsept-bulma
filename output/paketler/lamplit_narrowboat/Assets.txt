Light the lantern on an old painted narrowboat and drift along a quiet English canal through a long autumn night. A slow, calm 2 hour sleep story for adults, told in the second person.

The boat moves at the pace of a walk. You steer with one hand on the tiller, warm a kettle on a little black stove, and take tea and buttered bread at the hatch while bats cross the last of the dusk and a heron lifts out of the reeds. Then you tie up under the willows, bank the stove, turn the bench into a bed and settle under a wool blanket while the water laps at the hull. Nothing needs to happen. The night just carries on.
