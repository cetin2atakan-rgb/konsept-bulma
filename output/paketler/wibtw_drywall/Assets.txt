## DESCRIPTION
Knock on a wall in an American home and it sounds hollow; knock on a wall in Germany or the United Kingdom and it often sounds solid. This video traces that difference back to a single 1894 patent, a Chicago company, and a labor shortage during the Second World War that locked drywall in as the national default.
