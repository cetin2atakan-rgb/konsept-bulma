## DESCRIPTION
At the tip of Wisconsin's Door Peninsula is a strait so dangerous that French sailors called it La Porte des Morts, the door of the dead, and the whole county still carries the name. Under the orchards runs the same Silurian dolomite ridge that forms the ledge of Niagara Falls. Retired geography teacher Walter Hale counts down 25 geographic facts about Door County: Wisconsin's oldest lighthouse on Rock Island, the wave-cut caves of Cave Point, the tallest dune in the state, a Norwegian stave church rebuilt by hand, the largest rural Belgian settlement in America, and an island once home to one of the country's largest Icelandic communities.
