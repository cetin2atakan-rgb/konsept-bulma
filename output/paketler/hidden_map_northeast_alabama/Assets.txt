## DESCRIPTION
In Northeast Alabama a river spends almost its whole life on top of a mountain, cutting a canyon roughly 600 feet deep before it finally drops off the edge. Retired geography teacher Walter Hale counts down 25 geographic facts about Lookout Mountain and the country around Fort Payne: the town that once made more than half of America's socks, Cherokee writing hidden in Manitou Cave just before the Trail of Tears, Alabama's only ski slope, one of the state's tallest waterfalls, and the night in 1989 when Fyffe's own police chief reported lights in the sky.
