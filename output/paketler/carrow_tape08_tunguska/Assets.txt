## DESCRIPTION
The Carrow Institute is a work of fiction inspired by real events and decades of unexplained lore. The archive, its records officer, and its people are invented. Real historical persons appear in dramatized form.

June 1908, central Siberia: a blast that flattened eighty million trees, a decades-long search for a crater that was never found, and a single dense fragment this office quietly moved and never mentioned again. A found-footage mystery audio drama, recorded in a records office basement in 1977 — thirteen days after its records officer was taken into the annex upstairs.

Monday, the 15th of August, 1977. Twelve minutes past midnight.

Records officer Raymond Keel returns after a thirteen-day silence to tell you what little he can about the annex, a drawer number older than this office itself, and a file about the oldest event in this entire archive — one with a single unsigned name in the margin that he cannot explain.
