## DESCRIPTION
The Carrow Institute is a work of fiction inspired by real events and decades of unexplained lore. The archive, its records officer, and its people are invented. Real historical persons appear in dramatized form.

30 June 1908, central Siberia. Something exploded above the forest, knocked down some eighty million trees in a starburst pattern, and left no crater behind. Decades later, a survey recovered a small, dense fragment from the resin of a tree near the center. It was sent away for analysis, and no record of it appears again. A found-footage mystery audio drama, recorded in a records office basement in 1977.

Monday, the 15th of August, 1977. Twelve minutes past midnight.

Thirteen days after his last recording, Raymond Keel is back in the basement. He carried six folders up to the annex, handed them over, and came back down with a new clearance, a drawer older than this office, and one instruction: keep cataloguing. Tonight's file is the oldest event in the archive, and it has a name pencilled in its margin that appears nowhere else: "Ask Carrow before closing."
