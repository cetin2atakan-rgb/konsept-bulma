## DESCRIPTION
Southeast Oklahoma hides mountains that run east to west, against the grain of almost every other range in America, a Choctaw Nation capitol that outlasted the government it was built for, a cave with more legend than evidence, and a reservoir still argued over in federal court. Retired geography teacher Walter Hale counts down 25 remarkable geographic facts about the Ouachita Mountains, the Kiamichi and Red River valleys, and the Choctaw Nation lands that shape this corner of the state.
