## DESCRIPTION
That glass plate turning inside your microwave is not there to help the energy reach every side of your food, that is a myth. It is a 1960s fix for a problem engineers had been fighting since Percy Spencer's accidental 1945 discovery at Raytheon, and the real reason it rotates has to do with standing waves, not simple exposure.
