## DESCRIPTION
The Carrow Institute is a work of fiction inspired by real events and decades of unexplained lore. The archive, its records officer, and its people are invented. Real historical persons appear in dramatized form.

23 November 1953, over Lake Superior near the Canadian border. A ground radar station picks up an unidentified target and sends up a jet interceptor with a pilot and a radar operator aboard. The controllers watch the two blips close in on the scope, and then the log reads: "contact merged with target, single return observed, contact not reacquired following merge." There is no distress call. Days of searching find no wreckage, no oil and no crew. A found-footage mystery audio drama, recorded in a records office basement in 1977.

Saturday, the 17th of September, 1977. Twelve minutes past midnight.

Raymond Keel reads the most procedurally honest file in his drawer. The official explanation came years later: the target was a foreign transport plane that had strayed off course, and the pilot became disoriented. The government of that country has formally and repeatedly said its plane was never there. For the first time in a long while, Keel finds no pencil note in the margin, and he isn't sure what that silence means.
