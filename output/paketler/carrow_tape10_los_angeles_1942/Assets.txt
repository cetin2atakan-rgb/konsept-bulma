## DESCRIPTION
The Carrow Institute is a work of fiction inspired by real events and decades of unexplained lore. The archive, its records officer, and its people are invented. Real historical persons appear in dramatized form.

Los Angeles, the early hours of 25 February 1942. Eleven weeks after Pearl Harbor and two nights after a Japanese submarine shelled the coast, radar picks up something approaching from the sea. Searchlights find it, and for about an hour the city's guns fire more than fourteen hundred rounds into the sky. Nothing ever comes down. A found-footage mystery audio drama, recorded in a records office basement in 1977.

Monday, the 22nd of August, 1977. Twelve minutes past midnight.

Raymond Keel expected this to be the easiest file in his drawer to explain. Instead he finds a radar station serviced five days earlier, an hour of coordinated fire that stops and starts again, a newspaper photograph cropped around the one shape that mattered, and the founding charter of his own office, dated six weeks after this night and signed by no one.
