## DESCRIPTION
The Driftless Area is the one corner of the Upper Midwest the last ice age never touched, and across this episode we count down twenty-five remarkable geographic facts about it, from a buried meteor crater under a Norwegian college town to bird shaped mounds carved into river bluffs and the valley where America first learned to save its own topsoil. Along the way we visit the crookedest river in the country, a cave you can ride a boat through, and the coulee country Amish and Norwegian immigrant families have farmed for generations.
