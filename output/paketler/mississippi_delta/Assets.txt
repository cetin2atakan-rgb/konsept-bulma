## Description
25 surprising facts about the Mississippi Delta, from the ancient sea floor under its cotton fields to the levee break of 1927 and the birthplace of the blues.
A countdown through Mississippi Delta history and geography along Highway 61: Native American mounds, Mound Bayou, the Crossroads, Parchman Farm and more.

The flattest farmland in America was built by the Mississippi River one flood at a time, on top of a sea floor thirty-four million years old. On that same ground stand the Winterville and Jaketown mounds, Mound Bayou, the town founded by people who had been enslaved, the levee that broke at Mounds Landing, Parchman Farm, and the plantations, train platforms and crossroads where Charley Patton, W.C. Handy, Muddy Waters and B.B. King made the Delta the home of the blues.

Which fact surprised you most? If you live in the Delta, tell us what we missed.
