=====================================================
Stay after closing in a small Vermont general store on the night of the first hard frost, with a pot-bellied stove glowing in the middle of the floor and frost slowly feathering the big front window. A calm 2 hour sleep story for adults, told in the second person.

The last customer is long gone. You turn the sign, hear the door bell's one small note, and the warm room is yours: shelves climbing into the shadows, a coffee mill with an iron wheel, a wheel of cheese under a glass dome, barrels of apples and crackers, jars of hard candy, a hanging kerosene lamp, a wall clock, a checkerboard left mid-game, and a round gray cat asleep on a flour sack.

You feed the stove with split maple and warm the kettle, share a cracker and a slice of cheese by the fire, sweep the worn boards and dust the tins, refill the candy jars, cover the barrels, coil rope in the hardware corner and wipe the oil cans. You stand at the glass while the frost grows and a dog barks once far down the road. Then you wind the clock, lower the lamp, bank the stove and settle on a cot in the back room under a wool blanket, with the stove's glow in the doorway. Nothing needs to happen.

This video is meant for relaxation. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you have ongoing trouble sleeping, talk with your doctor.
The narration is a synthetic (AI-generated) voice and the pictures are AI-generated illustrations.
This is a work of fiction. The store, the village and everything in it are invented.
