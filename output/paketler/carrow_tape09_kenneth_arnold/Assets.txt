## DESCRIPTION
The Carrow Institute is a work of fiction inspired by real events and decades of unexplained lore. The archive, its records officer, and its people are invented. Real historical persons appear in dramatized form.

24 June 1947, Washington State. A private pilot searching for a missing military transport sees nine flat, crescent-shaped objects cross the face of Mount Rainier faster than any aircraft of the time could fly. He tells a reporter they moved like a saucer skipped across water. Within days, the whole country is talking about "flying saucers," a shape he never described. A found-footage mystery audio drama, recorded in a records office basement in 1977.

Thursday, the 18th of August, 1977. Eleven minutes past midnight.

Raymond Keel reads the file at the front of nearly every drawer in the building: the pilot's own sketch with its odd, darker ninth object, a speed worked out from two mountains on a chart, a second witness, and the hoax a few weeks later that cost two Army officers their lives. In the margin, in the same pencil hand as last time: "Carrow flagged this one first."
