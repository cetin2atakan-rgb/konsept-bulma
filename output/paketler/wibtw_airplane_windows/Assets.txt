## DESCRIPTION
Every airplane window you have ever flown past is rounded or oval, and that was not a design choice, it was a lesson learned the hard way in the early 1950s. This video traces the de Havilland Comet accidents of 1954, the Farnborough water-tank test that found the real cause, and the metal fatigue problem that quietly reshaped every pressurized aircraft built since.
