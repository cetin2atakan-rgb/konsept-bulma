## DESCRIPTION
The Carrow Institute is a work of fiction inspired by real events and decades of UFO and unexplained lore. The archive, its records officer, and its people are invented. Real historical persons appear in dramatized form.

April 1964, a gully outside Socorro, New Mexico: four measured depressions in dry soil, scorched brush, a hand-copied marking, and a government case file that was never closed with an explanation. A found-footage mystery audio drama, recorded in a records office basement in 1977.

Tuesday, the 26th of July, 1977. Ten minutes past midnight.

Records officer Raymond Keel reads the one file in his drawer that the government's own investigators listed, in writing, as unexplained. A skeptical captain's tape-measure diagram, a patrolman's radio transcript, and a clause in the arrangement that finally starts to come into focus.
