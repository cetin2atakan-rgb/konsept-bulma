## DESCRIPTION
The Texas Panhandle looks flat and empty from the interstate — but it's hiding the only plant in America that assembles nuclear weapons, a canyon deeper than you'd ever guess, and a fence line that once paid for Texas's entire state capitol. Retired geography teacher Walter Hale counts down 25 remarkable geographic facts about the Texas Panhandle, from the Ogallala Aquifer running dry beneath the wheat fields to Palo Duro Canyon hiding in plain sight behind the Caprock Escarpment.
