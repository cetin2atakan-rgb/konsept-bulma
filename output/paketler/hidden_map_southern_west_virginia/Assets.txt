## DESCRIPTION
A town named War. A mountain where private planes dropped bombs on striking coal miners in 1921. A river that some geologists argue is older than the Appalachians it cuts through. Retired geography teacher Walter Hale counts down 25 geographic facts about Southern West Virginia, from the New River Gorge Bridge and the ghost town of Thurmond to the Hatfield-McCoy feud, the Mine Wars, the millionaires of Bramwell, and the coal seam that fueled the U.S. Navy.
