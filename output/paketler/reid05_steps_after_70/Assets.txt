## DESCRIPTION
#StepCount #SeniorHealth #HeartHealth

Is the "10,000 steps a day" rule actually backed by science? In this video, we examine 5 facts about daily step counts that most adults over seventy have never heard clearly explained, including where the 10,000-step number actually came from, why more steps doesn't always mean more benefit, and the step counts where the benefit leveled off in large studies of older adults.

You'll learn about step-count thresholds, the diminishing-returns pattern in large mortality studies, gait speed as an independent health marker, sedentary bout length, and why breaking up long sitting periods may matter as much as your total daily steps.

You'll also learn two simple, research-based techniques — finding your personal baseline and breaking up long sitting periods — that take under a minute to understand. If you have a heart or lung condition that affects safe activity levels, these may be reasons to discuss your specific activity target with a healthcare professional.
