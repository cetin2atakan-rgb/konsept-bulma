## Description
You train often, yet your body barely changes. For many men the missing piece is not the program, it is how hard each set really is. Coach Hal walks through five effort mistakes and what the research says about how close to failure your sets should go.

We cover stopping sets too early, misjudging how many reps you have left, choosing weights that are too light, going to failure on every set, and repeating the same numbers for months. At the end you get one simple effort plan that works with any program.

General fitness education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if something hurts.
