## Description
A well built back reads from across the room, even in a plain shirt. Coach Hal covers five research-backed exercises that build both back width and thickness: pull ups and chin ups, the lat pulldown, the inverted row, the bent over row and the straight arm pulldown.

You will see why the endless grip width debate matters less than most lifters think, which row is easiest on your lower back, and the most common back training mistakes. At the end you get one simple weekly back plan.

General fitness education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if something hurts.
