## Description
Some men are not huge, yet they feel like steel. Behind every muscle is a tendon, and most lifters never train them on purpose. Coach Hal covers five research-backed rules for tendon training: why heavy loads matter, why isometric holds work, why tendons need months, how to keep muscle from outrunning tendon, and why jumping is not the same as tendon training.

At the end you get one simple weekly plan that fits any training split. This video is general training education and is not a treatment for tendon pain or injury.

General fitness education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if something hurts.
