## Description
After forty, many men assume getting weaker is just part of aging. Research says much of it comes from how they train. Coach Hal covers six mistakes that make men over forty look and feel older than they need to, and the simple fixes for each.

We cover giving up on serious strength training, training too light, skipping legs, never training speed and power, getting recovery wrong, and doing too little total work. At the end you get one weekly plan that works at home or in a gym.

General fitness education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if something hurts.
