## Description
Your first heavy set feels weaker than it should, and the reason often happened before you touched the bar. In this video Coach Hal walks through six pre-workout mistakes that research links to lost strength, and the simple fixes that cost nothing.

We cover long static stretches, skipping the warm-up, jumping straight to heavy weight, short sleep the night before, hard cardio right before lifting, and saving your most important lift for last. Every point is tied to a published review or study, listed below. At the end you get one simple pre-workout routine you can use from your next session.

General fitness education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if something hurts.
