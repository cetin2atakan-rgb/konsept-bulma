## Description
Some men walk into a room and people instinctively give them a little more respect. Much of that signal comes from the neck, the traps and the upper back, the muscles people read first. Coach Hal covers five exercises that build them, each tied to published research.

We cover the shrug, direct neck training, the farmer's carry, the overhead press and the deadlift, with simple form cues and safety notes for each. At the end you get one weekly plan that fits any training split. This is about looking capable and calm, not about picking fights.

General fitness education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if something hurts.

Sources:
