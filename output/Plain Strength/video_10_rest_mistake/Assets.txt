## Description
You finish a hard set and jump straight back under the bar, thinking short rest means a better workout. Research says that habit can quietly cost you strength. Coach Hal covers five rest mistakes and what the studies say about how long to rest between sets.

We cover resting too short on big lifts, rushing rest and losing reps, chasing the pump with short rest, using the same rest for every exercise, and wasting rest time on your phone. At the end you get one simple rest plan for any program.

General fitness education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if something hurts.

Sources:
