## Description
You train hard and press heavy, yet in a plain t-shirt your shoulders still look narrow. Coach Hal breaks down six mistakes that keep shoulders looking small, and the simple fixes that research supports.

We cover pressing without direct side delt work, forgetting the rear delts, too few weekly sets, training shoulders only once a week, ignoring the lats, and the posture habit that hides your width every day. At the end you get one simple weekly plan that fits any training split.

General fitness education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if something hurts.
