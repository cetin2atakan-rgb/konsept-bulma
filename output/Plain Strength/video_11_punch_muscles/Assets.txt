## Description
Some men with huge arms barely move a heavy bag, while a lean, skilled boxer makes it jump. A hard punch is a full body chain that starts at the feet. Coach Hal covers the five muscle groups behind punching power, what boxing research says about each, and how to train them for sport and fitness.

We cover the legs, the hips and trunk, the pushing muscles, the upper back and shoulders, and the forearms and grip, plus why skill and timing matter so much. This video is about bag work and athletic power, not fighting. Always use wraps and gloves and learn technique from a qualified coach.

General fitness education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if something hurts.
