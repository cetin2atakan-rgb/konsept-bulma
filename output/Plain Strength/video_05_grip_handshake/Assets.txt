## Description
In one second, a handshake tells people something about you. Research links a firm, complete handshake to better first impressions and even stronger hiring recommendations, yet many men today have weaker hands than men a generation ago. Coach Hal covers five exercises that build a grip people can feel.

We cover the dead hang, the heavy farmer's carry, the plate pinch, wrist curls and reverse curls, and pulling without straps, plus how to give a handshake that feels solid and confident without crushing anyone. At the end you get one simple weekly grip plan.

General fitness education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if something hurts.
