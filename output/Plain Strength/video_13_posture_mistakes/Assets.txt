## Description
Two men of the same size walk into a room, and only one looks strong. The difference is posture. Coach Hal covers six posture mistakes that make men look weaker than they are, from a forward head and rounded shoulders to slumped sitting, closed body language, the power pose myth and relying on reminders instead of strength.

Each point is tied to published research, and you get one simple daily and weekly plan at the end. This video is about looking and feeling stronger, not about diagnosing any back or neck problem.

General fitness education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if something hurts.

Sources:
