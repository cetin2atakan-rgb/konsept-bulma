## Description
You curl every week, yet your arms still look small in a t-shirt. A big part of your upper arm is not the biceps at all, it is the triceps on the back and the brachialis underneath. Coach Hal covers five research-backed exercises that build them.

We cover the overhead triceps extension, the close grip press, the pushdown, the hammer curl and heavy pulling with chin ups and pulldowns, plus the most common arm training mistakes. At the end you get one simple weekly arm plan.

General fitness education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if something hurts.
