## Description
You do push-ups almost every day, yet your chest still looks flat. Research shows push-ups can build muscle and strength much like the bench press, but only when they are hard enough. Coach Hal covers six push-up mistakes and the simple fixes for each.

We cover stopping far from failure, never making push-ups harder, random hand placement, half reps, sagging hips, and skipping the pulling muscles. At the end you get one simple weekly push-up plan that works at home with no equipment.

General fitness education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if something hurts.
